Open Stream — A: Quick list

Today's 380 px window, extended. Each person is one 28 px line; the window grows a row at a time, downward, as it does now (global.js, OFFLINE_ROW_HEIGHT). An offline person's broadcasts open inline. Static sample data; nothing here calls the network. The dashed outline marks IINA's title bar, which Streams doesn't draw.

Terms. J1, J2, J4 and J10 are approved user journeys (docs/design/USER-JOURNEYS-2026-11-22.md): J1 a stranger with no history, J2 the daily viewer who knows who they want, J4 the catch-up viewer after a VOD, J10 keyboard and VoiceOver use. D-034 is the decision in docs/DECISIONS.md that Twitch data comes from Helix (Twitch's documented API) first and otherwise from GQL (twitch.tv's internal interface, called anonymously). #74 and #76 are the GitHub issues for these suggestions and for the People screen. Frecency is a score of how often and how recently the viewer opened someone.

Try it

Type “oh”, use ↑↓, Return, →/← to open or close an offline person's broadcasts, Tab to reach them, ⌘I for why a row is here, and Escape to clear and then close.

The person row

Compact density: avatar or initial with the live dot on it, name, platform mark, one secondary line (category and title when live, newest broadcast when offline), then viewers or time since last live. The reason is never permanent text: it is the tooltip, the VoiceOver description, and the info button (⌘I). Shared with the People screen (#76), which would use this same row.

Keyboard and VoiceOver (J10)

KeyDoes
↑ ↓Moves the selection through people and, when expanded, their broadcasts. Focus stays in the field (a combobox driving a listbox through aria-activedescendant).
↩Live row: opens with chat. Offline row: opens its broadcasts and selects the newest. Broadcast: plays it with chat replay. URL or typed-name row: opens it.
→ ←On an offline row, with the caret at the end of the text: open or close its broadcasts.
Tab / ⇧TabInto the broadcast list of the selected offline person, and back to that person.
⌘IShows or hides why the selected row is here.
EscCloses the reason, then clears the field, then closes the window.
VoiceOverEach row reads as, for example, “Ohnepixel, live, 12,340 viewers, Counter-Strike, 1 of 6”, with the reason as its description. Position comes from aria-posinset/aria-setsize; groups are “Your People” and “Twitch search”.
