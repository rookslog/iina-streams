Shown first: search, then the Live tab: live channels, ranked, with viewers. The playing channel and its quality sit in a strip pinned at the bottom.

Behind a tap:

  • offline channels: the closed Offline group;
  • VODs: the VODs tab (continue watching, this channel, your channels' recent ones), or › on any row;
  • categories with live channels in them: the Categories tab;
  • chat on/off, chat settings, import, history controls and sign-in: the … menu on the bottom strip.

Order: search and tabs pinned at the top, the tab's list scrolls, the playing strip pinned at the bottom.

Trade: the most browsing for the 360 pt, at the cost of three tabs to learn; quality is one tap from anywhere.
