## 4. Readings of the want

Each reading is tied to their words, with what speaks for it and against it.

### R1. The moment, not the page (my working reading)
Opening the panel interrupts watching. The want is that the interruption be short, calm and person-first: one obvious act, with the rest reachable but quiet. The panel should know things without saying them, so order, brightness and size carry what headings and sentences carried.
- **For:** "I click the plugin menu button, see all the different options, click it, switch, and then collapse the thing back again" (06:44); "things I want to quickly check and focus on" (06:44); "a more minimalist way" (06:32); "you can have other ways of communicating that than just the text" (06:32); "too much text" (07:06); "trash" after a page of headed sections (07:19).
- **Against:** they also asked for VOD search, filter, sort and thumbnails (07:06), which is browsing rather than a quick switch, and for "a mini version of the dashboard".

### R2. Keeping up with people over time
What they keep returning to is time: picking up a VOD, catching what they missed, a person's back catalogue. The panel's subject would be people over time, not live versus offline.
- **For:** "I do like the continue watching thing. I do like that a lot" (07:06); "you watched them a long time ago… I want to go visit it again" (07:06); "I know they're not online, but I want their VODs. Or even if they are online, you may still want to watch their VODs" (06:32); "we need a better way of splitting between live and offline" (07:17).
- **Against:** "maybe we put Continue Watching at the bottom. I don't know" (07:19) ranks it below the people.

### R3. Beyond the browser
Copying Twitch's sidebar, at any quality, is replicating the browser. The want is something a web page can't do, such as speed, the keyboard, or not losing your place.
- **For:** the 08:34 words above; their reasons for IINA, "lightweight… smoother… keyboard… slicker" (08:34).
- **Against:** "just like what Twitch has" (06:47), and Twitch's sidebar categories, described approvingly (06:32).

### R4. A demand to be read, addressed to me
Much of the rejection is of being read literally by a lead who wasn't situated, and the mock was the evidence of that. On this reading, a layout close to round 2, done with care and at real fidelity, might be acceptable.
- **For:** tonight's words; the 06:44 frustration; the timing, after several misses.
- **Against:** "We might need to rethink this layout" (07:19) is about the layout itself.

### R5. A real mini dashboard
The want is breadth in a small space, a small Twitch, and the failures were in execution: spacing, crowding, the wrong controls.
- **For:** "a mini version of the dashboard"; "search… VODs… filter or sort them" (07:06).
- **Against:** "all that should be for the dashboard and not for the quick menu bar" (06:32).

### Through Lacan's distinction (a lens, see the warrant above)
- **Need:** get to another stream.
- **Demands:** what was said and addressed to me: search, VODs, thumbnails, continue watching, sort and filter.
- **Desire:** the remainder, what is still unmet when every demand has been met. Rounds 1 and 2 met the demands, roughly one section each, and were rejected. So the want is not the sum of the demands.

The readings above try to name the remainder, and none will name it fully. **Expect a remainder after the next round too.** Its complaints will be evidence, not a list of changes.
