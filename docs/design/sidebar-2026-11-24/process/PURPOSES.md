# The five directions and what each says it is for

Each is a design for the same surface: the IINA media player's plugin sidebar for the Streams plugin (Twitch and later other platforms). The panel opens over the video on the left edge, 300–500 pt wide (360 by default), is opened briefly, and is closed. Each direction states its own purpose; judge the purposes as well as the builds.

| Folder | What it says it is for | What it refuses |
|---|---|---|
| P | Keep up with your people over time: who's on now, what you missed, what you left unfinished. | Grouping by person, live and offline sections, settings |
| Q | (states none) | (states none) |
| R | Get from what you're watching to the next person in one click, then get out of the way. | Headings, sections, thumbnails in the list, settings, anything that isn't a person |
| S | Switching costs a keystroke, over the picture, like ⌘-Tab; the panel is only for looking through one person's broadcasts. Press Tab over the video. | Opening a panel to switch; lists of people in the panel |
| T | The panel is the stream you're in: this person, where you are in their broadcasts, and who else is on at a glance. | Being a directory of everyone; search as the main thing |

The data: real public Twitch channels, snapshot 24 November 2026; the viewer's watch history and half-watched positions are invented for the mock.
